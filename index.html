Who We Are

MORE THAN A COMPETITION.

Talented Star is a children's talent discovery platform built to give every child — regardless of background — a fair, safe, and memorable stage to shine.

🎯 Our Mission

To help children discover, express, and develop their talents while building confidence, creativity, communication, and stage presence.

Starting in Ujjain and expanding across Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and eventually the entire nation — one district at a time.

For Parents & Guardians

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
TO KNOW.

We understand that sending your child to a competition can feel overwhelming. Here's a simple guide to how it works — and we're always one WhatsApp away if you have any questions.

1

You Choose the Category

Pick from 7 talent categories. Your child competes only in their age group — no unfair matchups.

2

You Register & Pay ₹500

Fill the form with your child's details and yours. Pay the ₹500 fee and get a participant ID by confirmation.

3

We Share Event Details

You'll receive the venue, schedule, dress code, and performance rules well in advance.

4

Your Child Performs

Judged on published criteria — talent, creativity, confidence, presentation, and discipline. Full transparency.

5

Every Child is Recognised

All participants receive a certificate. Winners get their prize, trophy, and certificate on stage.

Is my child's personal data safe?

Yes. Your child's date of birth, phone number and address are never displayed publicly. Only authorised staff can access registration data.

Is the photo/video consent compulsory?

No — it's always optional and separate. Your child can participate fully without giving media consent.

What if my child is nervous on stage?

That's completely normal! Our team ensures a warm, encouraging environment. Every child is cheered — not just the winners.

Can I stay with my child at the venue?

Yes. Parents and guardians are welcome and encouraged to be present throughout the event for comfort and safety.

Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Who can participate? +

Children aged 5–16 across four age groups. Registration must be done by a parent or legal guardian. All backgrounds, schools, and cities are welcome.

What is the registration fee? +

₹500 per child, per category. This covers participation, a Participant ID, and a participation certificate (subject to attendance). Whether additional categories require extra fees will be confirmed in the official rules.

Can a child enter multiple categories? +

This will be confirmed in the official event rules. An additional fee may apply for each extra category. Check final rules before registering for more than one.

How are winners selected? +

Performance categories use a 100-point framework: Talent & Skill (30), Creativity (20), Confidence (20), Presentation (20), Discipline (10). Memory and Mind Games use separate accuracy-based scoring. The overall champion is selected via a transparent, pre-published method.

What prizes are available? +

Proposed: District Champion ₹10,000 · First Runner-up ₹5,000 · Second Runner-up ₹3,000 + category awards. Amounts are proposed and subject to official confirmation before the event.

When and where is the event? +

Event date and venue will be announced soon. Follow @talentedstar975 on Instagram or WhatsApp +91-8962836674 to get notified immediately.

Is my child's personal data safe? +

Yes. Children's personal details are never displayed publicly. Data is stored securely with access restricted to authorised staff. Photo/video consent is always separate and optional.

What is the refund policy? +

The refund and cancellation policy will be published before registrations open. Please review it carefully before making payment. Contact us on WhatsApp +91-8962836674 for any questions.

Can parents stay at the venue? +

Yes — parents and guardians are encouraged to be present throughout for their child's comfort and safety. The environment is designed to be warm, encouraging, and family-friendly.

How do I contact the organiser? +

WhatsApp or call +91-8962836674 anytime. DM us on Instagram at @talentedstar975. We aim to respond within a few hours.
